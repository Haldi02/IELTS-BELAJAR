🗣️ IELTS Speaking Complete Guide (20 points)

💡 Golden Rule for Speaking: Gunakan rumus A.R.E. (Answer + Reason + Example). Jangan hanya menjawab "Yes" atau "No". Perluas jawaban Anda secara natural.

🎤 Part 1: Introduction & Interview (5 points)

Durasi: 4-5 menit. Examiner akan bertanya tentang diri Anda, rumah, pekerjaan, atau topik sehari-hari.

Topic 1: Work & Studies

5 points

Q1: Do you work or are you a student?

Q2: Why did you choose to do that type of work (or study that subject)?

Q3: What is the most difficult part of your job (or studies)?

📝 Lihat Contoh Jawaban Band 9
A1: "I'm currently a final-year university student majoring in Business Administration. I've been studying there for about three years now."

A2: "Well, I've always been fascinated by how businesses operate and the strategies they use to succeed in competitive markets. Plus, I come from a family of entrepreneurs, so I grew up surrounded by discussions about business. I felt that this degree would give me a solid foundation for my future career."

A3: "I'd say the most challenging aspect is the workload during exam periods. It can get quite overwhelming juggling multiple assignments and revisions simultaneously. However, I suppose that's part of the university experience, and it has taught me a lot about time management and prioritization."

Topic 2: Hometown & Accommodation

5 points

Q1: Where is your hometown?

Q2: What do you like most about your hometown?

Q3: Has your hometown changed much since you were a child?

📝 Lihat Contoh Jawaban Band 9
A1: "I'm originally from Bandung, which is a bustling metropolitan city located in the western part of Indonesia, known for its cooler climate and vibrant culinary scene."

A2: "I'd have to say the culinary scene and the people. There's an incredible variety of street food and high-end restaurants alike. Plus, the locals are exceptionally warm and welcoming, which gives the city a strong sense of community despite its massive size."

A3: "Oh, absolutely. It has undergone a massive transformation. When I was younger, it was much greener and less congested. Over the past decade, there's been a surge in high-rise buildings and shopping malls, which has unfortunately led to heavier traffic, but it has also brought more economic opportunities."

🎤 Part 2: Long Turn / Cue Card (10 points)

Durasi: 1 menit persiapan, 1-2 menit berbicara. Examiner akan memberikan kartu topik.

Cue Card Practice
01:00
Describe a memorable journey you have taken.
You should say:
• Where you went
• Who you went with
• What you did there
• And explain why this journey was so memorable for you.
📝 Lihat Contoh Jawaban Band 9 (2 Menit)
"I'd like to talk about a trip I took to Kyoto, Japan, about two years ago, which remains etched in my memory. I went there with two of my closest friends from university during the autumn season, which turned out to be the absolute perfect time to visit because the maple trees were turning vibrant shades of red and orange.

Kyoto is an ancient city in the Kansai region, famous for its classical Buddhist temples, traditional wooden houses, and stunning gardens. We stayed in a traditional ryokan, which is a Japanese inn, and that in itself was a unique cultural experience. During our stay, we visited numerous historical sites, including the iconic Fushimi Inari Shrine with its thousands of vermilion torii gates winding up the mountain. We also took a traditional tea ceremony class and explored the Arashiyama Bamboo Grove, which felt almost otherworldly, especially in the early morning mist.

What made this journey so profoundly memorable for me was the perfect blend of history, culture, and natural beauty. Kyoto has this incredible ability to make you feel like you've stepped back in time, even though it's a modern city. The attention to detail in everything, from the meticulously maintained gardens to the exquisite presentation of the food, was truly inspiring. Furthermore, the locals were incredibly welcoming and patient with our clumsy attempts to speak Japanese. It was, without a doubt, one of the most enriching and eye-opening travel experiences I've ever had, and it truly broadened my perspective on different cultures."

🎤 Part 3: Two-Way Discussion (5 points)

Durasi: 4-5 menit. Pertanyaan abstrak terkait topik Part 2. Gunakan bahasa yang lebih formal dan berikan opini umum.

Topic: Travel & Tourism

5 points

Q1: Do you think tourism always benefits a country?

Q2: How has the way people travel changed in recent decades?

Q3: Should governments restrict the number of tourists visiting certain places?

📝 Lihat Contoh Jawaban Band 9
A1: "That's a nuanced question. On the surface, tourism brings significant economic benefits — it creates jobs, generates foreign exchange, and stimulates local businesses. However, I don't believe it's always beneficial. Over-tourism can strain local infrastructure, drive up housing prices for residents, and lead to environmental degradation. In some cases, the cultural authenticity of a place can be compromised when it's tailored specifically for tourists. So, it needs to be managed sustainably."

A2: "The transformation has been quite dramatic. In the past, international travel was largely a luxury reserved for the wealthy, and planning a trip required visiting a travel agent. Today, budget airlines have made flying affordable for millions, and the internet has revolutionised how we research and book trips. Platforms like Airbnb have also changed accommodation options. Additionally, social media has heavily influenced travel behaviour, with many people now choosing destinations based on their 'Instagrammability'."

A3: "I think in certain extreme cases, yes. Places like Venice, Machu Picchu, and certain national parks have faced severe over-tourism, which threatens their preservation. Implementing visitor caps, timed entry systems, or higher fees during peak seasons can help manage the flow and protect these sites for future generations. However, such restrictions need to be balanced carefully to avoid harming local economies that depend heavily on tourism revenue."
